# Expected-life table for fair wear (Appendix C)

Used **only to suggest** whether a change since check-in is chargeable. The officer decides; suggestion and decision are both recorded. Figures are starting points to agree with legal and finance; administrator-maintained in the system.

| Element | Expected life | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Interior decoration | 3–5 years | Most scuffing after two years or more reads as fair wear. |
| Carpet, mid-range | 5–8 years | Pro-rate replacement cost against remaining life, never charge full cost. |
| Vinyl / laminate flooring | 8–12 years | Burns, gouges and water damage are not wear. |
| Kitchen work surface | 10–15 years | Delamination at joints usually indicates water ingress, not wear. |
| Kitchen units | 15–20 years | Door and hinge damage is separable from carcass life. |
| White goods | 8–10 years | Record make, model and serial at check-in or replacement cannot be argued. |
| Bathroom sanitary ware | 15–20 years | Chips and cracks are damage; staining is usually cleaning. |
| Internal doors and ironmongery | 20+ years | Holes, missing handles and forced locks are damage. |

Charging rule: chargeable cost = rate-card cost × remaining life fraction (show the calculation); never full replacement cost.
